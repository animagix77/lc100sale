02 / BUTTONS WITH A PURPOSE

Low range. High ego.

L switches 4HI / 4LO. K toggles center lock. Use 4LO for climbing, the rocky ford, deep snow, and mud.

03 / WHEN CONFIDENCE WINS

Congratulations. You’re landscaping.

Try reversing out. Still stuck? Press R or Boards to deploy four traction boards on stable ground—even with a wheel hanging. The truck brakes automatically and holds still while they’re placed. A little rocking is fine.

Ease forward in 4LO + center lock. The boards put themselves away. Unlike your tools. Roll it over? Back to your last checkpoint. We saw nothing.

04 / MAKE CAMP

Take the scenic detour.

Tap the trail map for the full route; driving pauses while you look. Choose your first path after the intro and the next path when you reach its fork, follow the flags past the volcano and across the canyon, then finish at Sunset camp. Pitch a shelter where you choose and light the campfire. Progress saves at each flag; this expedition ends at camp. Two rocky river crossings, muddy climbs, deep snow and a basalt crawl across molten lava. Use 4LO on the rocks and the timber suspension bridge. Line up on the bank, keep centered and drive steadily across. The floor is literally lava. Your insurance agent was not consulted. Weather switches between the expedition and your local forecast.
