IN MY OWN WORDS

I went out for mulch.
Accidentally bought a classic.

This was my first truck. I bought her for big Home Depot runs, bags of mulch, and getting the kids safely to baseball and soccer practice. Apparently, youth sports require enough equipment to establish a small outpost.

We went on plenty of camping adventures and traveled up and down the East Coast through rain and heavy snow without problems. During my ownership, I never took her off-roading or rock crawling. Her job was family trips, camping gear and implausibly large Home Depot purchases. The rocks were allowed to mind their own business.

I wasn’t shopping for a future collector’s item. But in an age of small engines and turbochargers, this big V8 has entered her classic era. I bought a mulch transporter. The enthusiasts apparently had other plans.

— The current owner

How to buy this
magnificent mistake.

Congratulations. You’ve made it this far, which means you understand the appeal of a 100 Series, own tools, or both. Here’s how we turn mutual enthusiasm into a very large set of keys.

What to bring. Besides optimism.

Money our bank agrees is real.

Bank wire, cash, or a cashier’s/certified check verified directly with the issuing bank. No personal checks. For a cashier’s check, let’s meet at your bank or ours and verify it before handing over the truck or signed title.

You, officially.

Bring a valid driver’s license and your full legal name, address and contact information for the title and bill of sale. “The guy with the socket set” is excellent branding, less useful paperwork.

Your own insurance.

Arrange coverage on the Land Cruiser before driving on public roads. Our insurance is not your coverage after the sale. This part is your department.

A legal ride home.

We remove and keep our New Jersey plates at closing. You cannot drive home on our plates. Arrange your own legal registration/plates or valid temporary/transit permit where permitted—or bring a trailer, flatbed or tow truck.

Buying from out of state? Confirm requirements with NJ MVC and your home state before arriving. NJ MVC provides a temporary non-resident registration process; eligibility and paperwork need to be arranged with MVC, not guessed in the driveway. Check the NJ MVC pickup guidance ↗

What you get. Full disclosure included.

  • One 2004 Toyota Land Cruiser, one glorious 4.7L V8, and approximately 220,000 miles of history.
  • The original NJ title, properly completed and signed; keys; and two signed copies of the bill of sale, one for each of us.
  • The VIN, actual odometer reading at closing, agreed sale price and date recorded in the transaction paperwork.
  • The owner-recalled maintenance history and any other records available. Service receipts are not available.
  • An as-is truck with the known issues disclosed on this site. The three EVAP codes are still part of the deal.

Meet at the bank. Skip the plot twists.

  1. Meet and present payment. Preferably at your bank or ours, especially for a cashier’s check.
  2. Verify and receive funds. The bank verifies the cash/check or completes the wire; we confirm payment has been received.
  3. Complete the paperwork. Sign the title and both bill-of-sale copies, recording the final details.
  4. Keep copies. We each photograph or copy the completed paperwork and keep our signed bill of sale.
  5. Plates off. Keys over. We remove our plates; you take possession and transport her using your own legal registration/permit or arranged trailer/tow.

The truck and signed title stay with us until payment is verified and received. Enthusiasm is welcome. It is not a payment method.

Everyone keeps a paper trail.

You keep the original signed title, your signed bill of sale and keys. We retain our plates, a copy/photo of the completed title, our signed bill of sale, proof of payment, your name/address/contact details, and copies of the final transaction paperwork. Our plates will be transferred to another vehicle or surrendered to MVC as required.
