Full video brief

Animate the supplied beach reference into one continuous clockwise 360-degree camera orbit around the stationary grey 2004 Toyota Land Cruiser 100 Series. The camera follows a level circular path at constant radius, fixed focal length and constant height. Begin at the exact front driver-side three-quarter reference angle, travel around the driver side, rear, passenger side and front, then return exactly to the starting composition. Same sandy beach and dune shrubs throughout; maintain believable parallax and identical daylight. The beach stays fixed in world space while the view changes with the camera. Ocean is on the passenger side, dunes on the driver side. No cuts, fades, zooms, location changes, driving, wheel rotation or morphing. Preserve the TOYOTA-lettered grille, silver factory wheels, KM3 tires, modest OME lift, side steps and included black Malone crossbars. Rear spoiler is absent. Preserve the empty 2-inch rear hitch receiver. Remove people and camping gear. No typography or watermark. Preserve known wear; do not invent the location or appearance of missing roof-rack trim or unseen rust. Use the rear reference only for vehicle identity, never its parking-lot background. Full truck stays visible in every frame. Deliver a silent 16:9 MP4, approximately 16 seconds, and matching 9:16 composition if supported. A single uninterrupted orbit with first and last frames matching is the priority.
